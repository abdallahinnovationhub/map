About Our Mission

Encouraging Young Programmers is an educational initiative created to encourage young people to discover the world of technology and programming. We believe that every young person has the potential to become a programmer, software developer, website designer or technology innovator. Our mission is to make programming education accessible, understandable and free for everyone.
